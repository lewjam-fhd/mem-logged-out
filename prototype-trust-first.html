March 1943 • Long Beach, California

[High-Resolution Historical Photo - Factory Scene]

Complete Story - Nothing Truncated:

Emma Rodriguez worked at the Douglas Aircraft factory in Long Beach during World War II. She was one of thousands of women who joined the workforce while men served overseas. This photo was taken during her shift in 1943. Emma helped build C-47 transport planes and was proud of her contribution to the war effort. She worked alongside her friend Dorothy Martinez, and the two remained close for decades after the war ended.

Emma's Life Story

Written by Maria Chen (granddaughter), 2019

Complete Written Story - Fully Accessible:

Emma Rodriguez was born in 1922 in Texas and moved to Los Angeles as a teenager. During World War II, she answered the call to support the war effort by working at the Douglas Aircraft factory in Long Beach. There she met lifelong friends and developed skills that would serve her throughout her life.

After the war, Emma married Carlos Rodriguez and together they built a life in East Los Angeles. They raised three children, ran a small grocery store, and became pillars of their community...
