## Design System Summary

**Colors:**
- Primary: #0051C3 (FamilySearch blue)
- Primary Dark: #003D99
- Text: #333, #666, #999
- Background: #F5F5F5
- Card: #FFFFFF

**Typography:**
- Headings: 28px / 22px / 18px, weight 600
- Body: 15px, weight 400
- Small: 13px, weight 400

**Spacing:**
- Cards: 24px gap
- Padding: 16px / 24px / 32px
- Margins: responsive

**Components:**
- Cards: 8px radius, subtle shadows
- Buttons: 6px radius, primary blue
- Prompts: 12px radius, larger shadows
- Indicators: 8px radius, blue accent
