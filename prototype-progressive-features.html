Thomas O'Brien

Passenger Manifest, April 1905

[High-Resolution Ship Manifest - Fully Viewable]
Complete Document - Fully Accessible:

Thomas O'Brien departed from Queenstown, County Cork, Ireland on the ship "Celtic" bound for New York. He was 23 years old, traveling alone, with plans to join his brother Michael who had immigrated two years earlier. The manifest lists his occupation as "laborer" and shows he carried $12 when he arrived at Ellis Island on April 18, 1905.

Family Photo, June 1920

Thomas O'Brien with his wife Margaret and their three children at their home in Brooklyn, June 1920. By this time, Thomas had established himself as a skilled carpenter and the family had saved enough to purchase their first home. This photo represents the realization of Thomas's American dream - fifteen years after arriving at Ellis Island with just $12 to his name.
